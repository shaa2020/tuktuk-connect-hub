# Getting started — guide for businesses

1. **Create your account** at `/signup` (when registration is open) and click the link in the email.
2. **Create your business**: name, industry, time zone, language. You start on the Free plan.
3. **Secure your account**: scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
   Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…). Then go to Settings → **Recovery codes** and save them.
4. **Follow the setup checklist** on your Home page:
   - **Connect a channel** (Connected accounts):
     - _Telegram_: in Telegram open @BotFather, send /newbot, paste the token. Done.
     - _Website chat_: switch it on, copy the one-line code into your website before `</body>`.
     - _Gmail_: turn on 2-step verification in Google, create an App Password, paste it.
     - _WhatsApp / Instagram / Messenger_: needs a Meta developer app — follow the on-screen steps.
   - **Invite your team** (Team page) — choose Business Admin, Manager or Agent.
   - **Add business information** (Knowledge Base): prices, opening hours, policies. The AI uses it.
   - **Set up the AI assistant**: tone and instructions; choose the built-in AI or your own key.
   - **Review your plan** (Plan & usage).
5. **Daily work**: Inbox (reply from the account the customer used), Contacts, Leads, Bookings,
   Schedule (shifts, time off, clock in/out), Tasks.
6. **Automate**: Automations → start from a recipe (booking confirmation, review request,
   unhappy-customer alert, lead follow-up). Customer-facing messages are real — test them first.
7. **Away mode**: the AI Operator can answer while you're away. Turn off "Reply to guests" to make it
   draft replies that wait for your approval in the Inbox.
8. **Your data**: Settings → Data & privacy → export everything any time; you can also set a message
   retention period or delete the workspace (30-day grace).
